## Amplenote's Longevity Pledge

Software often fails to persist long-term, despite being relatively easy to build initially. The company emphasizes that note-taking apps become integral to users' cognitive processes, making sustained availability crucial.

### Pledge #1: Responsible Growth

This section critiques the typical VC-funded startup trajectory where pressure to exit within 5-10 years threatens longevity. Amplenote adopts a different approach: "we're building incremental profitability as adoption spreads through word-of-mouth."

The founders note 15+ years of experience maintaining balanced growth through their earlier venture, Bonanza.com. They commit to refusing investment "predicated on an implied exit or IPO."

### Pledge #2: Fix Reproducible Bugs Before Adding Features

The company prioritizes bug resolution over feature development to prevent tech debt accumulation. They created GitClear, a companion product, to measure and eliminate technical debt systematically.

### Pledge #3: Data Portability

All user content—including images, tags, and Rich Footnotes—can be converted to markdown for export. The company maintains a detailed, transparent security policy and regular encrypted backups to external sources.

### Pledge #4: Succession Plan

If operations become unsustainable, Amplenote pledges to open-source the codebase publicly. Additionally, they commit never to sell the company to acquirers unwilling to open-source it upon service discontinuation.

### In Summary

The conclusion acknowledges unpredictability while reaffirming commitment to ensuring "Amplenote, and your Amplenote content, will endure indefinitely."
