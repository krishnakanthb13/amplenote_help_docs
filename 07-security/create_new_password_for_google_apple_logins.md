# Login with email/password when connected to Google/Apple ID

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Security](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/create_new_password_for_google_apple_logins)

Users who create Amplenote accounts via third-party services (Google or Apple) can establish email/password access as an alternative login method.

## Steps

1. **Log out** - Click your profile name (top left) and select "Log out."
2. **Access password reset** - On the login page, select "Forgot password?" at the bottom right.
3. **Enter email address** - Provide the email linked to your Google account or Apple ID. This generates a password reset link valid for 24 hours across any device.
4. **Set new password** - Complete the reset process.

## Result

Once configured, you can log in from any device or browser using your email/password combination rather than relying exclusively on third-party authentication.
