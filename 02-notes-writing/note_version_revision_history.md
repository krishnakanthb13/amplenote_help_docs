# Note version (revision) history

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Notes & Writing](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/note_version_revision_history)

## Overview

Every note in Amplenote keeps a history of how it has changed over time. This feature is particularly useful when you need to examine details from tasks completed long ago, or investigate what happened to content you expected to find.

## How to access revision history

To view a note's version history, use the triple-dot icon and select **View revision history** from the menu options available in each note.

## What you'll see

Once the revision history opens, you gain access to a chronological list of previous save points. By clicking into any past version, you can view the note as it existed at that specific moment, including information about recently completed tasks from that time period.

## Revision frequency details

The system adjusts revision granularity over time:

- Initially, the application saves revisions every **10 minutes** by default.
- As time passes, the granularity of the revision history is gradually reduced.
- For older content spanning months in the past, this eventually scales down to **one revision per day**.
