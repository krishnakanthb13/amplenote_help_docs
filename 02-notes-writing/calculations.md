# Calculations (expression/evaluation): Dates, time and math

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Notes & Writing](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/calculations)

## Overview

Amplenote enables users to calculate relative dates, mathematical formulas, and generate tables of contents by enclosing expressions in curly braces `{}`. When you type `{1+1}`, the system evaluates it and stores the formula in a Rich Footnote, displaying the result as `2`.

**Important:** You must manually type the closing `}` bracket to trigger evaluation.

## Date & time calculations

### Relative dates

- `{Today}`
- `{Tomorrow}`
- `{Yesterday}`

### Absolute dates

- `{Monday}`, `{The weekend}`, `{September}`
- `{October 31st}`, `{Oct 31}`
- `{End of March}`, `{Beginning of April}`
- `{First monday of September}`, `{Last friday of December}`

### Past and future dates

- `{Next monday}`, `{Last week}`, `{Next year}`
- `{In 14 days}`, `{A month ago}`, `{End of next month}`

### Time expressions

- `{Now}`, `{10 minutes ago}`, `{In three hours}`
- `{9 pm}`, `{21:30}`

### Combined date & time

- `{Today at 8pm}`, `{Tomorrow at 10:45}`
- `{Tuesday 22:00}`, `{Mar 12 8am}`

## Math calculations

Examples include:

- `{1+1}`
- `{pi*10**2}`
- `{(1+1)*(12/36)}`

## Recurring rules (for task details)

These expressions are used for task recurrence, not within curly brackets:

- Every 2 days, Every 3 weeks, Every 3 weeks on Friday
- Every weekday, Every 2 months, Every month on the last Friday

## Table of contents

Paid subscribers can insert `{toc}` to generate a hierarchical list of note sections that can include Rich Footnotes.
