# Outlining with collapsible bullets, tasks, and headings

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Notes & Writing](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/outline_with_collapsible_bullets_tasks_headings)

## Introduction

Amplenote supports hierarchy-based note taking, similar to apps like Workflowy and Roam. This document describes the various affordances available for organizing information hierarchically.

## Outlining in bullets, numbers, and tasks

You can create nested hierarchies combining tasks with bullets and numbered lists. The feature works by typing `* ` for bullets or `[] ` for checkboxes at the beginning of a line, then using Tab/Shift-Tab to adjust indentation.

### Key hotkeys

| Hotkey | Action |
|--------|--------|
| `Tab` | Indent |
| `Shift-Tab` | Outdent |
| `Ctrl-,` | Expand or collapse a list item with children |
| `Ctrl-Enter` | Toggle between bullet and task |
| `Shift-Ctrl-Up` | Move item up among siblings |
| `Shift-Ctrl-Down` | Move item down among siblings |

## Outlining with headings

Headings provide clear structural organization, particularly beneficial for longer essays or ongoing research. Collapsing heading content helps you focus on specific sections.

### Collapse methods

1. Hover over a heading and click the collapse arrow.
2. Position the cursor on the heading and press `Ctrl-,`.

![Hover on a heading, then click the icon to collapse is one way to get the job done](https://images.amplenote.com/fea0f5c8-2807-11ee-8a5a-4e62ba9bf71c/941155b9-28c6-4552-a95a-3b15abbef52f.gif)

### Collapse behavior

When collapsing headings, content hides until reaching either:

- A heading equal to or larger than the collapsed heading.
- A section divider (created by typing dashes at the start of a line).

### Special behaviors

- Pressing Enter at a collapsed heading's end automatically adds a hidden divider.
- Pressing Enter mid-heading splits it into two lines, keeping the bottom collapsed.
