# Removing Amplenote branding from published content

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Sharing & Publishing](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/remove_amplenote_branding_published_notes)

## What the branding looks like

For notes published and viewed on Amplenote's domain, content displays Amplenote branding (shown in a trial subscription example image).

![Viewing a note that was published by an Amplenote trial subscription](https://images.amplenote.com/5a24f68e-60f4-11eb-bad5-2a844617cbef/38bc3a53-329a-4cd5-97b1-82d4d574029a.png)

For embedded note content, a more compact treatment appears, with text appended at the bottom stating: "The content above is from a note published by an Amplenote subscriber. As updates to the note are made, they are reflected here in real time."

## How to remove the branding

The branding only displays during the trial period. Once you upgrade to a paying account, the note style options menu (referenced in "Publishing notes with background color and image") provides toggles for:

- Author information
- View count
- Branding elements

These toggles allow you to customize what appears on the public version of the note.

![Note style options menu for removing Amplenote branding from a published note](https://images.amplenote.com/5a24f68e-60f4-11eb-bad5-2a844617cbef/42cb4622-d66b-4502-b748-85e20e56924a.png)

![Toggling author information, view count, and branding elements on a published note](https://images.amplenote.com/5a24f68e-60f4-11eb-bad5-2a844617cbef/12a0309e-abf6-4933-965b-4b0e1dd0d031.png)

![Published note appearance after removing Amplenote branding](https://images.amplenote.com/5a24f68e-60f4-11eb-bad5-2a844617cbef/0958a7d1-b24d-48a2-8e65-5ba5126cfec2.png)

**Key takeaway:** Trial accounts display Amplenote branding on published notes, but once the account has upgraded to become a paying customer, branding removal becomes available through the styling options.
