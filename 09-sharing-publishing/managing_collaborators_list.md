# Managing collaborators list

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Sharing & Publishing](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/managing_collaborators_list)

When you access the Amplenote sharing dialog, a list of frequent collaborators appears. You can manage who displays in this list by selecting "Manage collaborators" from the "Invite friends" page.

![Viewing Amplenote collaborators](https://images.amplenote.com/bdb51e56-4c1f-11ec-bae5-4a72aa41d28b/e08d5b14-7ab5-4e02-8ca9-e70924d2a555.jpg)

## Active collaborators

Once you navigate to the collaborators management page, you'll see everyone you've exchanged notes with. The primary section shows active collaborators, which can be toggled as favorites or hidden from your view.

![Active collaborators section with favorite and hide toggles](https://images.amplenote.com/d980e5d0-4c78-11ec-be97-4a72aa41d28b/44494712-d836-482f-915f-e7c34d1e2fba.jpg)

## Additional sections

The page also includes:

- A section for past collaborators.
- A section for hidden collaborators.

![Past and hidden collaborators sections](https://images.amplenote.com/d980e5d0-4c78-11ec-be97-4a72aa41d28b/3be6bf42-cb10-47f5-9812-6a5a9eec2553.jpg)

These organizational categories allow you to control visibility and access to your collaboration history across different relationship statuses with your contacts.
