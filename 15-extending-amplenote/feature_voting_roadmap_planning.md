# Amplenote Roadmap via Feature Voting: How We Choose What to Work On Next

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Extending Amplenote](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/feature_voting_roadmap_planning)

## Overview

Amplenote employs a democratic approach to product development, blending user feedback with founder vision. The company maintains that "every user of Amplenote is a part-Founder of it, because they are influencing what gets built."

## Two Primary Voting Systems

### Public Voting Board

- **URL:** https://amplenote.featureupvote.com
- **Access:** Open to anyone
- **Vote Limit:** One vote per feature, unlimited total votes
- **Expiration:** No expiration on votes or suggestions
- **Purpose:** Identifies features for broader audience appeal
- **Goal:** Implement at least two top-10 features per release

### Supporter Voting Board

- **URL:** amplenote.com/suggested_features
- **Access:** Limited to active supporters
- **Vote Limit:** Up to 15 total votes, maximum 4 per feature
- **Expiration:** Votes and suggestions expire if activity drops
- **Purpose:** Prioritizes needs of paying customers sustaining development

## Additional Input Sources

**Founding Team Vision:** The "Idea Execution Funnel" guides strategic direction, emphasizing task and calendar integration as differentiation points.

**Community Channels:** Email, Discord, and ad hoc reports inform bug fixes, which receive "high likelihood of developer attention within a week or two."

## Philosophy

Amplenote aims to become the consensus choice for users seeking integrated task, calendar, and note-taking functionality. The company emphasizes building "an app that reflects the needs of its user base" with demonstrable execution on voted features.
