# Opening PDFs, Youtube videos, and other content in Amplenote

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Extending Amplenote](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/inline_open_pdf_youtube_more)

## Adobe PDFs

To view PDFs in Amplenote, upload them by dragging into a note or using the "Attachment" icon. Once uploaded, the PDF appears as a collapsed object. Clicking the PDF title opens it inline. Alternatively, hold Cmd (macOS) or Ctrl (PC) while clicking to open it in the sidebar Peek Viewer, which you can resize for better readability while taking notes.

## Youtube videos

As of 2024, there are two methods for viewing YouTube content. First, copy a video's URL, select text in Amplenote, and paste the URL to create a Rich Footnote. When expanded, this displays the video inline.

For a larger view, hold Cmd (macOS) or Ctrl (PC) and click the "video" link to open it in the Peek Viewer at your preferred width. "Peek Viewer access is available to all trial users, and users on a Pro plan or above."

## Viewing other types of content inline

Reference the Rich Footnotes page to see supported content types. As of 2024, Amplenote can preview "content from X/Twitter, Google Sheets, and JSON data." Microsoft Office attachments can be uploaded but cannot be previewed in-browser without opening them externally.
