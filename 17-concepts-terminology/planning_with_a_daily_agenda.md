## Planning with a Daily Agenda Template

Amplenote provides multiple planning approaches. While the [Idea Execution Funnel](https://www.amplenote.com/help/idea_execution_funnel_explained) is generally recommended, the Daily Agenda offers a practical "hybrid funnel" model for those whose schedules are unpredictable.

## Daily Agenda: Why?

Despite having a comprehensive calendar, inbox, and task list, users may adopt a Daily Agenda because it serves as "a scratchpad on which to ephemerally collect tasks sourced from the calendar and a variety of notes."

The Daily Agenda excels when pre-scheduled tasks prove impractical to complete. As the most recently updated planning document, it can supersede unrealistic schedules. The document typically divides into two sections:

1. **Three core goals** — tasks that define a successful day
2. **Per-hour time estimates** — projected time allocation

The Daily Agenda operates with "a much lighter touch than the calendar." Incomplete items don't generate reminders on subsequent days, offering flexibility for dynamic schedules.

## Creating a Daily Agenda

While [creating and copying a template](https://www.amplenote.com/help/using_note_templates) is technically optimal, users can simply update the previous day's version. The approach emphasizes function over form: "There is no process nor glory in the Daily Agenda."

Users are encouraged to experiment with their own formats and share successful versions with support@amplenote.com for potential inclusion in documentation.
