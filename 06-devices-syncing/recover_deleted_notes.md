# Recovering recently deleted notes

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Devices & Syncing](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/recover_deleted_notes)

## Overview

If you accidentally deleted a note within the past 30 days, you can potentially recover it through a couple different avenues.

## On desktop: the "Deleted" label in three-pane view

Expand your desktop view so that you can see all three panes. A "Deleted" folder will be available below your tag list.

![Deleted folder in three-pane view](https://images.amplenote.com/44a303be-d00f-11ed-927c-d2ab91c23399/5e1b9654-a9b8-4a81-8793-ed57cff29a0a.png)

## Search `group: deleted` tag

"If you enter `group:deleted` within your Amplenote search bar, you will see notes that have been recently deleted on the current device"

![Search group deleted results](https://images.amplenote.com/44a303be-d00f-11ed-927c-d2ab91c23399/bd5b40cc-a0dc-453a-a12e-7e1fc5e7213f.png)

## On mobile: expand options to see "Deleted" option below tag list

Tap the triple line in the upper-left of your app to expand the sidebar area. The "Deleted" label is at the bottom of this list.

![Mobile deleted option in sidebar](https://images.amplenote.com/44a303be-d00f-11ed-927c-d2ab91c23399/2991c6a2-cb1f-4cec-b35c-322333d65e26.png)
