# How to define what is "Important" and "Urgent"?

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Organize Everything](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/define_urgent_and_important)

## Overview

Amplenote allows users to apply "Urgent" and "Important" labels to tasks. "Urgent" tasks experience rapid Task Score increases, while "Important" tasks show steady progress up the leaderboard.

## Preferred definition

According to Amplenote's founders:

- **Urgent**: Tasks that need completion "in the next few days. The sooner the better."
- **Important**: Work that "aligns closely with your long-term interests" and makes completing it feel like a productive day.

## Examples

**Urgent tasks** (typically time-sensitive):

- Test preparation spanning 60 minutes, due end of week
- Responding to communications from significant contacts
- Resolving checkout technical issues

**Important tasks** (supporting long-term wellbeing):

- Monthly outreach to family members or appreciated contacts
- Health and nutrition improvement opportunities
- Building passive income streams

Individual interpretations may vary ("Ymmv") depending on personal circumstances and priorities.
