# Idea Execution Funnel

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Organize Everything](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/idea_execution_funnel_explained)

## Overview

The "Idea Execution Funnel" describes how an idea progresses from initial capture through consideration to scheduling. The funnel's objective is helping users "align your intentions with your actions," ensuring daily choices reflect life priorities.

![Idea Execution Funnel: how ideas evolve in Amplenote](https://images.amplenote.com/7cd14f88-0ccb-11ec-b829-427796570f24/9939863b-db9a-4bb8-8891-75ab303053b3.png)

## Four phases of the funnel

### Phase I: Write/Capture — "Jots" in Amplenote

The first phase prioritizes frictionless idea capture. Popular capture tools include Raindrop.io, MyMind, Google Keep, Apple Notes, and Bear.

Amplenote's solution, called **Jots**, minimizes formatting distractions. The interface "skips the niceties of formatting toolbars, titles and tags" to maintain focus on creation itself.

![Jots gets out of your way to focus on creation. Shown here in two-pane mode](https://images.amplenote.com/ca37a49a-ec04-11ea-be19-e25d04d7a2b3/e4d98413-fc59-44d2-abb1-9e2a531a5e4d.png)

### Phase II: Edit, Refine, Organize, Share — "Notes" in Amplenote

Phase two filters the volume captured in phase one. Tools like Notion, Evernote, OneNote, and Roam format content for sharing or publishing.

Amplenote's **Notes** mode enables backlinking between ideas and hierarchical organization through tagging. Users can choose explicit structure (favored by Zettelkasten enthusiasts) or organic connection patterns.

![Notes gives you tools to edit, organize, share, or publish](https://images.amplenote.com/ca37a49a-ec04-11ea-be19-e25d04d7a2b3/a2c7f742-fdb5-4b3f-9b75-428964b2b950.png)

### Phase III: Make It Actionable — "Tasks" in Amplenote

Most note-taking apps provide inadequate task management. Specialized tools like Things, Todoist, and Google Tasks address this gap.

Amplenote's **Tasks** mode extracts tasks from notes by tag or date range. The system includes built-in recurrence and an "Eisenhower matrix" framework for prioritizing important work over urgent distractions.

![Tasks mode allows browsing to different productivity centers in your life](https://images.amplenote.com/ca37a49a-ec04-11ea-be19-e25d04d7a2b3/77c77e44-30a9-401d-8eaf-7d8a08911975.png)

### Phase IV: Get It Scheduled, Get It Done — "Calendar" in Amplenote

Scheduling separates intention from execution. Amplenote's **Calendar** supports drag-and-drop task scheduling and syncs with Outlook, Google Calendar, and Apple Calendar while preserving event coloring.

Users establish "Task Domains" to separate personal and professional calendars, enabling context-aware scheduling suggestions.
