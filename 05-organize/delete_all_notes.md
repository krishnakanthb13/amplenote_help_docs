# Deleting all notes

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Organize Everything](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/delete_all_notes)

## Overview

You may want to delete all notes to start fresh. Common reasons include reimporting an exported notebook without duplicates, or reimporting a smaller subset of previously exported notes.

## Finding "Delete all notes"

The delete function is located in Account Settings. Navigate to **Account Settings → Account & Password**, where you will find the button to delete all notes.

![Clicking the "Delete all notes" button under "Account Settings" -> "Account & Password"](https://images.amplenote.com/4583b61c-60f3-11eb-877b-2a844617cbef/e5026a0f-7fc7-4874-8824-bdd9f7fac24e.png)

> 💡 **Tip:** To delete only specific notes, refer to the tag management documentation for instructions on "Deleting all notes with a certain tag."

## Using "Delete all notes"

The deletion process requires account verification:

1. Enter your account email address.
2. The "Delete All My Notes" button will become active.
3. Click the button to immediately mark all notes as deleted in the database.

![The button to "Delete all notes" activates after entering the account email address](https://images.amplenote.com/4583b61c-60f3-11eb-877b-2a844617cbef/44dc2173-57d2-4aed-a560-e151d12a76f6.png)
