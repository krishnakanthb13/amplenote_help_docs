# Accountability Goal Groups Overview

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Goals, Forums & Social](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/join_an_accountability_goal_group_features_overview)

## Main Sections

**Join an Accountability Goal Group**

Users can join groups up to their subscription limit and optionally place a 10-100 coin wager on goal completion. According to the guide, "If you successfully remember to mark the goal as complete as often as the goal calls for... you will earn a 20% return on your coins bet for every month you remain in the goal."

Failing to mark completion within the recurrence interval plus grace period results in coin forfeiture and group removal.

**Dormancy Phase after Sign Up**

A waiting period follows signup and each completed cycle during which the "Goal Complete" button is unavailable. This prevents abuse and aligns with natural goal completion rhythms.

**Mark Progress in an Accountability Goal Group**

The critical required setting is "recurrence interval" (in days, max 60), determining how often members must check in. Users should set calendar reminders and record completions with substantiating notes and advice for peers.

**Create an Accountability Goal Group**

Available to Pro+ subscribers. Goals require a name and description (supporting markdown or public note tokens).

**Goal Configuration Options:**
- Recurrence interval (3-30 days typical)
- Goal end date (6 months default, up to 1 year maximum)
- Recurring meeting cadence with video conference URL
- Maximum member limit

The guide emphasizes that "people can improve their follow-through if they have a community that celebrates their progress."
