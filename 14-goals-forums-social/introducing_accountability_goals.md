## 📈 Accountability Goals

Amplenote is launching two research-backed features to improve goal followthrough by closing the gap between intention and action.

**Important caveat:** Choosing goals thoughtfully is essential. The concept of "shiny goals" describes goals that sound appealing due to novelty (like joining a gym after hearing a fitness podcast) but often lose momentum when the initial enthusiasm fades. The article recommends contemplating new goals for at least one hour before committing, and ideally journaling for an hour to clarify your thinking.

### Tactic #1: Social accountability

The first feature helps users discover like-minded people pursuing similar goals. Research indicates that having "a specific accountability appointment with a person you've committed to" dramatically increases completion likelihood. Accountability Groups allow creators to designate recurring weekly or monthly meetings to build real human connections among members.

### Tactic #2: Risk/reward with coins

The second feature introduces "coins," a currency system. Pro+ subscribers can risk 10-100 coins betting they'll complete their goal. Participants earn 20% "interest" monthly for staying on track by marking goals complete within the creator's recurrence interval.

Coin redemption options include:
