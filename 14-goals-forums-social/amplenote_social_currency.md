# Amplenote Currency ("coins")

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Goals, Forums & Social](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/amplenote_social_currency)

Amplenote Social enables users to earn coins through various activities. As stated, "Coins have no real monetary value, but you can redeem them for an assortment of benefits."

## Trading coins for perks

The platform offers these redemption options (subject to change):

| Coins | Perk |
|-------|------|
| 100 | Post to promo forums |
| 500 | Sticky promo post (1 day atop list) |
| 1,000 | T-shirt mailed within US |
| 5,000 | T-shirt mailed to EU or Japan |
| 5,000 | Free month of Pro |
| 10,000 | Free month of Unlimited |
| 100,000 | Four hours of programmer time |

The team indicates they plan to expand this offerings throughout 2025 and welcomes community suggestions.

## Earning coins

**Subscription coins:** Monthly allocations based on tier—Pro (10), Unlimited (25), and Founder (100).

**Daily coin redemption:** Visiting the Amplenote Social home page allows users to "earn 10 coins" daily, with variation by account level (5 for Personal, 20 for Founders).

![Redeeming daily coins for Founder-level subscriber](https://images.amplenote.com/c608e496-c874-11ef-b1df-af034016e911/7436ed48-0d49-4926-a987-4fc32c9012f9.gif)

**Accountability Goal follow-through:** Users may wager up to 100 coins per goal and receive "a 20% 'interest' payment on the amount you wagered" monthly if completed on schedule. Incomplete goals result in forfeited coins.

**Subscriber appreciation reactions:** Positive reactions from Unlimited or Founder subscribers on forum posts or goal updates award 5-10 coins per upvote.
