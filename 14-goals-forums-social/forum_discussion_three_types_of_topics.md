# Forum discussions: three types of forum topics

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Goals, Forums & Social](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/forum_discussion_three_types_of_topics)

Amplenote provides community members with three distinct forum categories for interaction and collaboration.

## Sharing, tips, and greeting forums

Non-promotional conversation spaces are located in the social page's left column. General-purpose forums include:

- Share & Showcase
- Plugins & Integrations
- Knowledge Management/PKIM
- Templates & Advice Lists
- New User Introductions
- Productivity, GTD, Lifestyle-Centric Planning
- Games & Fun
- Weekly/monthly Reviews

## Promotional topics

These forums help entrepreneurs connect with potential customers. "Posting a new topic in the promotion forums requires some coins in order to keep our signal-to-noise ratio as high as possible."

## Accountability Goal topics

Each Accountability Goal includes a dedicated forum for members discussing tactics and progress. Users must join the group before posting.

## Terms and Conditions for Forums and Goals

Prohibited content includes:

- **Cryptocurrency Promotions** — Any blockchain or token discussions
- **Gambling Content** — Casino, sports betting, or gambling platform promotions
- **Exploitative Content** — Scams, predatory behavior, or content targeting minors
- **Spam and Schemes** — MLM promotions, fake job postings, crowdfunding requests without verification, pyramid schemes
- **Harassment or Hate Speech** — Bullying or discriminatory remarks based on protected characteristics

Violations may result in content removal, suspension, or permanent account banning.
