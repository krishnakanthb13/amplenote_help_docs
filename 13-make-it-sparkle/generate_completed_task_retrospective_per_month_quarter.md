# Project Retrospectives: Stylish, Shareable Breakdown of What Got Done

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Make it Sparkle](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/generate_completed_task_retrospective_per_month_quarter)

The Quarterly Retrospective is a visual celebration of what you got done in the past quarter. It's inspired by Spotify's Year in Review to add a mix of different visual treatments on the categories of progress you've traversed.
