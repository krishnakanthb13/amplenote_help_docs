## Video uploads

### Two types of video uploads

Amplenote provides two ways to surface video content within notes.

#### 1. Within Rich Footnotes: Youtube, Uploaded Videos, and animated gifs

You can embed a YouTube video or uploaded video nearly anywhere using Rich Footnotes. Select text, press CMD-K (macOS) or Ctrl-K (PC), and insert a URL or drag a video or animated gif.

![Embedding a video within a Rich Footnote](https://images.amplenote.com/73efbaa4-1242-11ec-9068-f674aa3b173c/fd4bddcb-0497-4186-a527-77815ddc3a20.gif)

#### 2. In the note body: Uploaded videos and animated gifs

Videos can also be inserted directly into the note body as either videos or animated gifs. Animated gifs work best for content 20 seconds or less without sound. Insert videos or gifs by dragging them from files into the note or clicking the "Image or Video" icon in the toolbar.

![Inserting a video or animated gif into the note body](https://images.amplenote.com/73efbaa4-1242-11ec-9068-f674aa3b173c/752f2e82-2916-4f58-9a05-1e4b5a3a48bf.gif)

![An uploaded video shown in the note body](https://images.amplenote.com/73efbaa4-1242-11ec-9068-f674aa3b173c/d094734b-838c-4d9c-8f77-189842c6d1fd.png)

### Unplayable videos

Sometimes videos uploaded on one device cannot play on another. This occurs due to video codec differences. iPhones default to HEVC (H265) codec, which only Safari and Edge support as of 2021. Chrome and Firefox cannot play HEVC videos. To resolve this, convert iPhone videos to H264 codec via QuickTime or set H264 as your iPhone's default codec.

![Message that a video can't be played](https://images.amplenote.com/73efbaa4-1242-11ec-9068-f674aa3b173c/39ad210a-dab1-4548-8db6-ce338c69dd8f.png)

### Use cases for videos

Common applications include:

- **Help/How-to content** — explaining concepts with fewer words
- **Web research capture** — preserving page interactions using Amplecap
- **Meeting notes** — recording brief summaries instead of lengthy minutes
- **Bug reports** — efficiently communicating issues to developers
- **Journaling** — creating vivid personal records with visual elements
- **Blog posts** — breaking up text with visual variety

![Using a video to explain help and how-to content](https://images.amplenote.com/73efbaa4-1242-11ec-9068-f674aa3b173c/1b5c726c-af19-438a-b087-be570bbc2557.png)

## Image uploads

Images can be inserted directly into note bodies or within Rich Footnotes using the same methods as videos.

### Inserting images using Amplecap

The Amplecap Chrome and Firefox browser extension simplifies frequent screenshot insertion into notes.

### Image options: captions, alignment, dimensions, OCR

#### Image captions

Add captions via the triple-dot menu. Captions feature distinct styling, inherit image alignment settings, appear in published notes, copy with images, support standard formatting and Rich Footnotes, and allow multi-line text using Shift-Enter.

![Adding a caption to an image via the triple-dot menu](https://images.amplenote.com/73efbaa4-1242-11ec-9068-f674aa3b173c/59201baf-ef0f-45c5-bdcc-125777f94e57.png)

![Image captions are styled differently and support the usual text formatting options](https://images.amplenote.com/b330cb30-23b3-11ed-9dd5-327ebed86db8/07eccfe0-cacd-4a54-93d1-9d95290676c7.jpg)

![An image displayed with its caption](https://images.amplenote.com/73efbaa4-1242-11ec-9068-f674aa3b173c/20b4348f-d093-489e-86d9-d61e4bab5a1e.jpg)

#### Image alignment

Use the triple-dot menu to select left or center alignment for images.

![Choosing left or center alignment for an image](https://images.amplenote.com/73efbaa4-1242-11ec-9068-f674aa3b173c/310d6ba8-6bef-4aa8-b609-2c0e73fc30ed.png)

#### Image dimensions

Resize images by dragging the bottom-right corner or entering pixel dimensions manually via the triple-dot menu.

![Resizing an image by dragging its bottom-right corner](https://images.amplenote.com/73efbaa4-1242-11ec-9068-f674aa3b173c/20f5f0a9-7dbc-4579-bbc6-fdc2d2a6fd31.gif)

![Entering pixel dimensions for an image via the triple-dot menu](https://images.amplenote.com/73efbaa4-1242-11ec-9068-f674aa3b173c/8e6c8824-d62a-4590-b105-6e0ce9a8d674.png)
