## How to use paid Amplenote features without actually paying in 2023

In early 2022, Amplenote released a permanent Free version featuring core capabilities aligned with their "Idea Execution Funnel" vision. By December 2022, the platform offered three paid plans unlocking features like Note Publishing and expanded storage.

For power users unable to afford paid subscriptions, Amplenote provides several mechanisms to access premium features at no cost:

### New as of 2023: Become a Plugin Developer

Developers can access paid features through plugin development opportunities. Additional details are available in their plugin development guide.

### Become an Amplenote Scholar or Videographer

Contributing educational content earns free subscription months:

- **Amplenote Scholars** write and share educational articles about productivity and knowledge organization using Amplenote, including Help Center submissions
- **Amplenote Videographers** produce educational videos on Amplenote usage

Contributors receive Unlimited plan months, extra feature voting privileges, and a profile badge.

### Become a member of the Booster Club

The Booster Club comprises volunteers promoting Amplenote online. Members receive one month of Unlimited upon signup, continued rewards for activity, a special profile badge, and extra voting privileges.

### Become a Community Helper

Active participants in Amplenote's Discord and Reddit communities answering user questions receive monthly Unlimited subscriptions as periodic awards.

### Invite someone to Amplenote

When referral signups occur for paid subscriptions, referrers gain a free Unlimited month, profile badge, and extra voting privileges.
