# Improve research & thinking depth via Rich Footnotes

## Introduction

The article opens with a quote from George Boole about language as an instrument of human reason. The author argues that better writing tools enable deeper thinking by allowing writers to explore perpendicular topics through footnotes, accumulating connections between ideas that are otherwise difficult to hold in mind simultaneously.

## 🧠 Creative potential limited by brain space

The piece contends that writing quality is constrained by how well a writer understands their subject. However, this presents an exploitable opportunity: human cognition itself is the limiting factor.

The author notes that humans operate with significant cognitive constraints—"20 watt, 1 kilogram blob of gelatin" running evolutionary software. When ideas become sufficiently complex, fitting everything into one's brain simultaneously becomes impossible.

The key insight offered: "Great writing tools make ideas of incredible complexity possible to contemplate by substituting discipline for intellect." Writing serves two functions—taming complexity and enhancing precision. Even brilliant thinkers benefit from externalizing ideas through writing rather than relying purely on memory.

## ✍️ Writing and thinking rely on one another to become their best self

The creative process involves cycling between rough ideas, writing tools, and refined writing to produce better ideas that persuade others.

The article emphasizes that writing tools must enhance creative energy. Tools like Microsoft Word inhibit rather than support articulation of complex technical ideas due to limitations in equation formatting, code display, and mobile functionality.

## 🎩 Writing in 3d with Rich Footnotes

Rich Footnotes represent Amplenote's core feature for intensifying communicative power. They compress information into concise representations while capturing idea essences.

### Use case examples:

**Instructions for peers**: Visual guides can be condensed into a single sentence with embedded images rather than spanning multiple pages.

**Vacation planning**: Property details, prices, and location information appear inline with footnoted images showing arrival times and distinctive features.

**Work research**: Charts and data comparisons are footnoted rather than interrupting narrative flow.

**Reminders to future self**: Quick reference links for technical configurations and preferences.

**Bugs to work on later**: Issue tracking integrated within documentation.

**Email to todo conversion**: Emails become structured task items with associated context and actions.

**Jokes**: Parenthetical asides or extended punchlines.

The article advises against placing essential details exclusively in footnotes—they should enhance rather than contain core information. Readers interested in deeper exploration can access supporting materials.

## Writing as thinking

The hypothesis returns to its foundation: better writing tools enable better thinking. Rich Footnotes add a visual dimension to text, allowing complex ideas to be expressed concisely while providing optional depth for interested readers. This leverages cognitive abilities collectively, enabling effective transmission of intricate concepts.
