# How the CEO of Amplenote chooses what to work on every day

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Use Cases](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/how_the_ceo_of_amplenote_chooses_what_to_work_on)

## Overview

The CEO of Amplenote shares their personal productivity system for managing 5,000+ tasks. The approach combines high-level organizational thinking with practical Amplenote features to balance important versus urgent work while maintaining mental energy.

## Core Philosophy

The author explains that choosing what to work on isn't purely about discipline—it requires understanding context. Key factors include:

- Current energy and willpower levels
- Dependencies on clients, employees, or teammates
- Creative capacity versus execution readiness
- Balancing "important" with "fun" tasks to maintain sustainable productivity

The underlying principle: mixing strategic importance with spontaneity and pleasure creates both satisfaction and energy.

## Daily System Components

### Starting Point: Daily Jot and Calendar

The CEO maintains a daily jot limited to 10 items for top-of-mind tasks. They check the calendar each morning to confirm scheduled events and refresh memory on pre-planned intentions.

### Inbox Note Review

An "Inbox Note" serves as the collection point for:
- Unfiled tasks from discussions or spontaneous ideas
- Small tasks (1 hour or less) feasible any given day
- Important items warranting daily visibility

The CEO keeps this note under 50 tasks using three strategies:

1. **Move tasks to project-specific notes** using the `!move` command
2. **Snooze tasks** via `!hide`/`!snooze` for 10, 20, or 30 days
3. **Dismiss tasks** with the keyboard shortcut, knowing important items resurface naturally

![My inbox note is a mishmash of unfiled tasks at the top, followed by up to 50 tasks that I haven't hidden or moved to another note](https://images.amplenote.com/da3cf216-dad0-11ec-ba30-9abeb6de1996/518fbac0-deae-4f11-8523-d44348490133.png)

### Planning and Execution

After reviewing email, Inbox Note, Slack, Daily Jots, and calendar (approximately 20 minutes), the CEO identifies priority work. When planning ahead, they use the Peek Viewer to sketch the day into categories, sometimes dividing work into three segments.

![Sometimes it is useful to split the day's aspirations into 3 categories](https://images.amplenote.com/da3cf216-dad0-11ec-ba30-9abeb6de1996/1b32672a-44d4-412b-9b94-4cd2c62c0f18.png)

### Calendar Integration for Progress Tracking

Throughout the day, completed tasks get added to the calendar. This practice provides:
- Brain-saving record of work completed
- Material for weekly team updates
- Data for "meta-optimizing" time allocation and activity ratios

The `!move` and `!duration` Task Commands enable quick color-coded calendar logging without leaving the keyboard.

## Task Score: The System's Foundation

Amplenote's Task Score feature ranks tasks by analyzing past decision patterns. The algorithm considers:

- Frequency of task review
- Task complexity and execution time
- Importance designation

This provides a data-driven baseline for determining "best to work on today," though it requires context for optimal decision-making.

## Managing List Overflow

**Comfort thresholds triggering action:**
- Red tasks in "Work" Task Domain: 10 maximum
- Overall Inbox Note tasks: 60 maximum
- Daily Jot items: 10 maximum
- Today's plan note: 5 maximum

When thresholds are exceeded, the CEO addresses overflow through dismissal, snoozing, or resetting task scores rather than forcing completion.

### Guilt Management

The CEO emphasizes not feeling guilty about long lists, as overflow is "the default state of any daily to-do list that hasn't been pared back lately." Regular evaluation of which tasks can be dismissed is essential.

## Idea Capture Systems

**Mobile:** Quick Task Bar on Amplenote's mobile app

**Web:** Amplecap browser extension for creating todos from screenshots, URLs, and annotations

**Desktop:** Dropping new tasks directly into the Inbox Note

**Communication channels:** Email priority inbox (maximum 10 items, cleaned weekly) and Slack (tasks copied to todo lists if not immediately actionable)

## Long-Term Projects

The CEO uses flexible recurring todos for sustained multi-week or multi-month goals, marking them Important with a 60-minute maximum duration to reduce activation friction.

## Evolution and Adaptability

The author notes their approach "has changed plenty" over 12 months, cycling between minimal list usage and using lists as primary decision tools for business opportunities and resource allocation.

---

## Key Takeaway

Productive people learn to "zoom out and meta-optimize" their time. Tracking how different activity ratios (strategizing, coding, inbox management) correlate with satisfaction enables continuous refinement of what deserves attention each day.
