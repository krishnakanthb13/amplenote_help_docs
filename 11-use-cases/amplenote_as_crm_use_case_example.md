## Use case: Remember everyone you've ever met by using Amplenote as a Personal CRM

Amplenote can function as a Personal CRM system for maintaining connections with people you've encountered. The platform demonstrates one possible organizational approach for managing personal contacts.

**October 2021 update:** A dedicated video tutorial on this topic is available, created by Shu Omi.

Productivity expert Shu Omi provides guidance on using tags to organize contacts from different areas of life. The tutorial demonstrates how to structure and categorize your personal network.

![Shu showcasing one possible organization system for remembering personal contacts](https://images.amplenote.com/8b472584-c6ed-11eb-9663-7efd079009b0/fa48df4a-6e70-4d4e-8031-18430d9aa92d.png)

## Key recommendations:

- Consider prefacing people's names with `@` for quick browsing through your contact list using the "Quick Open" navigation feature
- Make liberal use of backlinking to remember the contexts and situations in which you've previously connected with specific individuals

This organizational approach helps you maintain meaningful relationships by keeping important details and connection history readily accessible within your notes.
