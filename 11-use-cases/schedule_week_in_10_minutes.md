# Schedule the week in under 10 minutes

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Use Cases](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/schedule_week_in_10_minutes)

Productivity expert Shu Omi demonstrates how to schedule a week efficiently using Amplenote. "This 7 minute video explains how he does it using Amplenote."

The approach involves combining engaging tasks with routine "barnacle tasks" to ensure important work gets completed alongside necessary but less exciting activities.
