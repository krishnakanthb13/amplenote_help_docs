# Are student/academic pricing discounts available?

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Account & Subscriptions](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/student_academic_discount)

Students, teachers, and researchers comprise a significant portion of Amplenote's user base, with features designed specifically for academic use. The company previously distributed free subscriptions to academics but changed this approach in June 2022 due to resource constraints.

## 📚 Amplenote Personal edition is permanently free for academic use

Rather than offering individual discount codes, Amplenote provides a robust "Personal" subscription at no cost. As of Q4 2022, this tier includes:

- Collaboration through note/tag sharing with individuals, groups, or public web access
- Rich footnotes functionality
- Calendar view for visual organization
- Amplecap browser extension for web content capture
- Hierarchical tags for project/class separation
- iPad handwriting and drawing capabilities
- Bidirectional linking with backlink filtering
- iOS/Android share sheet for mobile content submission

Users can simply "sign up for Personal" without special application. The page references a video tutorial by Shu Omi demonstrating iPad note-taking strategies.

## 📯 Another option for a free subscription

Students wanting additional features can apply to the **Amplenote Booster program**, which provides select free Unlimited subscriptions to those committing approximately "15 minutes per month helping to spread the word about Amplenote."
