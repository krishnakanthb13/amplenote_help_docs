# Setting a theme to change editor coloring

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Account & Subscriptions](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/appearance_theme_coloring)

If you want to customize Amplenote's editor colors, you can choose from 10 different color themes available to subscribers.

## Finding Theme Options

Access your theme choices through the "Appearance" tab in Account Settings.

## Available Themes

The following themes are listed by name and minimum subscription level required:

**Classic** (Personal) — The default blue-ish style provided when you first sign up for Amplenote.

**Minimal** (Pro)

**Arctic** (Pro)

**Pine** (Pro)

**Plum** (Pro)

**Honeybee** (Pro)

**Cabernet** (Unlimited)

**Eclipse** (Unlimited)

**Fuchsia** (Founder)

**Retro** (Founder)

## Mobile Availability

This feature launched on desktop in early May 2022, with a planned mobile app rollout by the end of June 2022. Your selected theme applies across all devices, though light/dark mode choices are device-specific.
