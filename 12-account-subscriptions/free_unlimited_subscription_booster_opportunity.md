## Free Unlimited Amplenote subscription opportunity

While many users find the Personal version sufficient, others seek the comprehensive features of an Unlimited subscription, including note publishing, tag sharing, calendar syncing, and Vault Notes.

However, financial constraints—particularly for users in non-dollar-based countries where pricing conflicts with local wages—can prevent upgrades.

For these users, an alternative exists.

In 2023, Amplenote launched the **Amplenote Boosters program,** enabling users to dedicate 10-20 minutes monthly to promote Amplenote in exchange for a complimentary Unlimited subscription. More information is available at the [Booster Application page](https://www.amplenote.com/booster_applications/new).

## Active or wait list?

The Booster membership maintains "low double digits" capacity, meaning slots may be unavailable when applying. Applicants without current openings can join a waitlist. The company prioritizes admitting enthusiastic applicants "more sooner than later."
