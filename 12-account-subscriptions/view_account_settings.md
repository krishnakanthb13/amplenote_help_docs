# View Account Settings

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Account & Subscriptions](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/view_account_settings)

## Overview

The **Account Settings page** serves as "the gateway to much of Amplenote's configurability" and controls access to numerous configuration options throughout the platform.

![A partial sampling of help topics that are controlled by visiting Account Settings](https://images.amplenote.com/c1381fbc-4c22-11ec-bae5-4a72aa41d28b/0f5377eb-0905-419a-a4cd-9a71d9191926.jpg)

## Desktop/Web (+ iPad for PWA Users)

On desktop, access Account Settings by clicking your account avatar located in the upper-left corner of the interface. This method works in both multi-column layouts and narrow pane configurations.

![In Jots and three column mode in desktop](https://images.amplenote.com/c1381fbc-4c22-11ec-bae5-4a72aa41d28b/8889e083-bc91-431f-abdf-203a18badbd6.gif)

![Accessing Account Settings in narrow panes on desktop](https://images.amplenote.com/c1381fbc-4c22-11ec-bae5-4a72aa41d28b/5f8671f8-59f0-4824-a325-122ef730fc62.gif)

## Mobile

On mobile devices, Account Settings can be found in a comparable location within the interface navigation.

![Accessing Account Settings on mobile](https://images.amplenote.com/c1381fbc-4c22-11ec-bae5-4a72aa41d28b/4925abad-9c30-48cc-adc9-9d5d94cce428.gif)
