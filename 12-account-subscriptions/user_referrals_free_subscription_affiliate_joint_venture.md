# User referral program & affiliates

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Account & Subscriptions](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/user_referrals_free_subscription_affiliate_joint_venture)

If you've gotten value from Amplenote, the platform wants to incentivize sharing with friends and business collaborators through their referral program.

## Getting credit for inviting users to Amplenote

Users can access their referral code by clicking their avatar, then selecting "Share Amplenote." When a referred user subscribes to Amplenote, the referrer receives:

- "A complimentary month of an Unlimited subscription"
- An Amplenote t-shirt opportunity
- A user badge on their public profile

## Referring multiple users

The platform tracks referrals beyond the first user. For each additional person who becomes a paying subscriber through your code, you earn "an additional free month of Unlimited subscription."

## Amplenote affiliates (joint ventures)

Currently, there's no formal affiliate program, though one is planned. However, users who refer at least 20 paying subscribers can email support@amplenote.com to receive "50% of what we earn from their first year as Amplenote subscribers."

The company reserves the right to deny compensation for "nefarious methods" and won't process affiliate payments for referrals under 20 paying subscribers due to administrative costs.

Custom referral URLs are available for users with large followings who wish to promote Amplenote publicly.

## Large following inquiries

Influencers, content creators, or business leaders interested in partnerships should contact the CEO directly.
