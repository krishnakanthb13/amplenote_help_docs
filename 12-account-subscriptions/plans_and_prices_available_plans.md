## Subscription Plans & Pricing

### What plans are available?

Amplenote offers four subscription levels: one free plan and three paid tiers.

**Personal (Free)**
Ideal for students, international users, and those needing core features without calendar syncing. Includes access to all four modes of the Idea Execution Funnel.

**Pro**
Designed for multitaskers who need the Peek Viewer sidebar, calendar synchronization with Google/Outlook/Apple, and collaborative tag-based note sharing.

**Unlimited**
Suited for users wanting to publish notes publicly, utilize Vault Notes for enhanced privacy, and customize their interface appearance.

**Founder**
For those supporting accelerated feature development, seeking early access to new capabilities, and wanting increased influence on product direction through enhanced voting power.

![The four Amplenote subscription tiers: Personal, Pro, Unlimited, and Founder](https://images.amplenote.com/600d8fe2-52cc-11eb-a9d1-3e0332af7e7b/1d50f7b9-518a-4c67-9cc3-303e6463a543.png)

### Does Amplenote offer a free plan?

Yes. The Personal plan is robust and free, offering unlimited device syncing and access to all Idea Execution Funnel modes. Users receive a "14-day free trial" with features matching the Pro plan upon account creation.

![Selecting the Personal plan on mobile](https://images.amplenote.com/600d8fe2-52cc-11eb-a9d1-3e0332af7e7b/a00005a3-2821-49b6-9acf-24da52670c84.png)

### How do I sign up?

Visit the Plans & Pricing page during your trial period. Upgrading instantly enables selected plan features, though billing begins only after your trial expires. Payment requires credit or debit card information with monthly or annual billing options.

![Checkout page with payment options](https://images.amplenote.com/aa40ff3e-030c-11e9-8d9b-fa2e15571acc/6f9002cd-76a7-4539-a9cf-13665234b45c)

### How will I be billed?

Billing occurs monthly or annually based on your selection. Amplenote accepts debit or credit card payments only. Failed payments restrict new note creation but preserve account access for viewing and exporting existing content.

### How do I change or cancel my plan?

**Web signups:** Access Account Settings → Subscription → "Update subscription" to modify your tier or billing frequency.

![After clicking 'Update subscription' you have the opportunity to upgrade or downgrade](https://images.amplenote.com/600d8fe2-52cc-11eb-a9d1-3e0332af7e7b/b5e44aad-6883-4c4f-a807-2e5a77ccb018.png)

![Canceling an Amplenote account after having signed up via Amplenote website](https://images.amplenote.com/600d8fe2-52cc-11eb-a9d1-3e0332af7e7b/7bc65a5f-4a82-451b-a4e0-96dfc838e3a3.png)

**App Store signups:** Use Apple or Google's native subscription management tools.

![Canceling subscription via App Store](https://images.amplenote.com/600d8fe2-52cc-11eb-a9d1-3e0332af7e7b/a94b8b4b-3752-4538-9136-949ce2382f7b.png)

![Canceling an Amplenote subscription on Android](https://images.amplenote.com/600d8fe2-52cc-11eb-a9d1-3e0332af7e7b/f2407dec-f3ec-4ef0-a0e3-4059c17f5e78.png)

**Cancellation:** Your plan reverts to Personal (free) upon cancellation. "Annual subscribers with 6+ months remaining" may request refunds through support@amplenote.com.
