# Deleting your Amplenote account

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Account & Subscriptions](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/delete_amplenote_account)

## Overview

By default, Amplenote accounts expire and are deleted after the trial period unless you add a credit card and select a subscription plan. However, users can manually delete their account to remove encrypted notes and attachments from Amplenote's servers more quickly.

## Finding the "Delete my account" Option

The deletion option is accessible through Account Settings. Users can locate the "Delete account" button on the default settings page by opening "Account Settings."

## Deleting Your Account

To delete your account, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to the "Delete my account" form
2. Enter your Amplenote email address to confirm deletion
3. Click the confirmation button
4. You will be logged out with account data removed from the database

**Important note:** "Images and attachments for deleted accounts are purged once per hour, and may remain in the edge cache for up to 24 hours (depending on your location) after being purged from storage." Any active subscription will be canceled simultaneously with account deletion.
