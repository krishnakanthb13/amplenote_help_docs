# Amplecap: Capturing Web Content with Our Chrome/Firefox Extension

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Capture: Jots & Amplecap](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/amplecap_browser_extension_guide)

## Introduction

Once Amplecap is installed, activate it using `Ctrl-Shift-A` when your browser is focused. This shortcut may differ if you use Firefox or have conflicting extensions.

You can customize the hotkey and explore usage tips in the Getting Started guide.

## 📦 Where Can I Get It?

Download options:
- **Chrome**: [Google Chrome Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/amplenote-web-clipper/jhmjlpdljfogaclolecijklcicciafaj/)
- **Firefox**: [Firefox Browse Add-Ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/amplecap-beta/)

### Displaying as a Browser Icon

By default, extensions don't appear in your browser bar. To add Amplecap's icon:

**Chrome/Brave**: Follow the visual guide provided to pin the extension to your toolbar.

**Firefox**: Click the engine icon and select "pin to toolbar."

## ⚡️ Quick Bookmark in One Step

Use the keyboard shortcut `Ctrl-Shift-E` to capture the current page as a task in a predefined note. On first use, select your destination note; change it anytime in extension settings.

## 🥅 Capture Content in 3 Steps

### Step #1: Choose the Capture Type

Select from four capture modes:
- Screenshot Selection
- Highlights
- Full Page
- Bookmark

Use `Up` and `Down` arrow keys to navigate options efficiently.

### Step #1.2: Choose How to Send Content

Two options are available:

**Copy to Clipboard**: Content goes to your Amplenote clipboard buffer for manual pasting later. You can skip Step #3 with this option.

When copying, choose whether to paste content directly into the note body or add it to a Rich Footnote first.

**Send to Amplenote**: Proceeds to Step #3 for automatic placement in your notes.

Use `Left` and `Right` arrow keys to switch between these options.

### Step #1.3: Choose Annotation Text

Enter custom text to replace the default annotation (page title) when capturing.

### Step #2: Select Content to Be Captured

- **Full Page or Bookmark**: No selection needed
- **Screenshot Selection or Highlights**: Manually choose content from the webpage

### Step #3: Choose the Destination

This appears only when you selected "Send to Amplenote."

**New Note**: Creates a brand-new note with captured content. Optionally change the title and assign tags.

**Choose Note**: Search for an existing note. Amplecap inserts content at the beginning.

### Step #3.2: The 3 Content Formatting Options

1. **Note Body**: Places captured content directly into a new note or at the beginning of an existing one.

2. **To-do Item**: Saves content as a task for future follow-up.

3. **Bullet Point**: Adds content to your lists within a Rich Footnote.

**Rules for to-do and bullet items**:
- Added to the beginning of existing notes
- Content stored inside a Rich Footnote using your annotation text

## Amplecap Capture Modes

### Screenshot Selection

Drag your mouse to select any part of a webpage. Click "Save selection" to capture the screenshot.

All uploaded images are processed by OCR, making text within screenshots searchable and extractable within Amplenote.

### Highlights

Manually select multiple phrases from a webpage and save them in one click.

Individual excerpts are editable before capturing. Formatting depends on destination:

- **Note Body**: Excerpts appear as a quoted block
- **Bullet or Task**: Excerpts stored in a Rich Footnote labeled "Excerpts"

### Full Page

Save a screenshot of an entire webpage to your notes.

### Bookmark

Save the URL of your current page to your notebook for reading lists or reference collections.

## Importing Amplenote Templates

On `public.amplenote.com/*` URLs, Amplecap enables one-click note duplication. Click "Duplicate Note" to create a copy in your notebook—the quickest way to import templates.

## Omnicapture

Available in Chrome, Omnicapture lets you create notes and send them to Amplenote from anywhere while your browser is open—the fastest way to add tasks.

**To enable**: Add a global shortcut in Chrome settings (see customization guide).

You can:
- Change the formatting of captured content
- Change the destination of content

Unlike other modes, Omnicapture doesn't capture webpage information.
