# iOS/Android Share Sheet: Sending text, links, images and PDF files on mobile

## Overview

Amplenote's native Share Sheet functionality enables users to send various content types to their notebooks directly from mobile devices. The older iOS Shortcuts approach is no longer maintained.

## How to Share Content to Amplenote on Mobile

The basic process involves tapping the Share button within an app and selecting Amplenote from the available options. The location of this button varies by application and operating system.

## Pinning Amplenote in the Share Sheet

### Adding Amplenote as a Shortcut on iOS

Users can pin Amplenote as a quick access option in the iOS Share Sheet. Video instructions are available.

### Pinning Amplenote on Android

Similar pinning functionality exists for Android devices, with accompanying video guidance.

## Editing Content Before Adding

When sharing content, a modal interface appears allowing users to edit the material before it's saved. "All of the usual formatting options are available."

## Supported Content Types

The Share Sheet functionality supports:

- Image files
- PDF files
- Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files
- URLs
- Text selections
