# Capture tasks via Apple Watch

> [← Help Index](../00-index.md) · Category: [Capture: Jots & Amplecap](./index.md) · [Source ↗](https://www.amplenote.com/help/apple_watch_task_capture)

## Installation

To capture tasks via your Apple Watch, use [Amplenote's Siri integration](https://www.icloud.com/shortcuts/c0183f6b3ddb480a89d64ecb36783e35). This shortcut uses Siri to quickly capture a task to your daily jot.

Upon visiting the link (on iOS or iPadOS) and clicking the "Get Shortcut" button, you will be asked to configure the shortcut:

![Configuring the Amplenote Siri shortcut](https://images.amplenote.com/0a8f7c12-cbe1-11f1-b8f0-6f215c2cd406/2333684d-51fa-4f4a-a605-a03041600ec9.jpg)

The only thing you need to set up here is the tag of your daily jots, usually `daily-jots`.

## How do I use the Siri shortcut once installed?

Long-press your power button on your iPhone or the digital crown on your Apple Watch to invoke Siri. Once Siri answers to you, say "Capture to Amplenote", then pause. Siri will understand that you want to capture a task to Amplenote and will ask you for the contents of your task. At this point, dictate your task to Siri, and you are done!
